Saving voice recordings

A grown-up needs to read and agree to this.

  • Saving keeps the voice recordings in this song, along with its beat and settings.
  • They are stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded or sent to anyone.
  • Anyone who uses this browser on this device can open and play the saved songs.
  • Only save voices of people who are happy to be recorded. For a child, that means their parent or caregiver agrees.
